Who it’s for

Brokerages, real-estate teams and developers whose agents can’t call every web, portal or listing-sign lead back straight away.

The problem

A buyer fills in the form on your listing at 10:02 on a Saturday. Your agents are at open houses. By the time someone calls back that evening, the buyer has spoken to another agent.

How it works, for one lead

  1. 10:02:14The buyer submits the form on your website.
  2. 10:02:51Property Connect AI calls back, triggered by the form.
  3. 10:04It answers questions about the listing: price, square footage and HOA fees.
  4. 10:06It checks budget, pre-approval and timeline, briefs the agent privately, then joins the buyer to the call.
  5. Sat 11:00The tour is booked, and the buyer gets an SMS invite with directions and the agent’s contact card.
