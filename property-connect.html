What it does

Speed to lead

  • Dials out as soon as a form is submitted, through a webhook
  • Takes inbound calls around the clock from listing signboards and marketing numbers
  • Answers listing specs: price, square footage, HOA fees, schools
  • Covers weekends, open houses and out-of-hours

Qualification and tours

  • The same questions every time: budget, mortgage pre-approval, move-in timeline
  • Spots buyers who also need to sell their current home
  • Books private showings straight into the agent's calendar
  • Sends SMS calendar invites with directions and the agent's contact card

Transfer and records

  • Warm transfer: the agent is briefed before the buyer joins the line
  • Script guardrails and fair-housing compliance maintained by Optimity
  • Full call recordings, word-for-word transcripts and sentiment tracking
  • CRM sync with Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, HubSpot, Salesforce or custom webhooks

How it runs for brokerages and teams

Real-time warm transfer

A telephony bridge connects qualified buyers to your agents' mobiles, with a private whisper briefing before they're connected.

One qualification standard

Every lead is asked about financing, timeline and location the same way, so agents don't chase unvetted leads.

Every call on record

Each call is recorded, transcribed and analysed for buyer sentiment, objections and agent follow-up.

We maintain it

We configure, tune and maintain the conversation flows and compliance guardrails. You show properties and close deals.
