What we take on

It’s selective. These are the things we look for before we say yes.

  • A real operational workflowSomething your team does every week, such as handling a kind of enquiry or moving information between two systems.
  • Your existing systems stayWe connect to the ERP, CRM, phone line, calendar or database you already use. Nothing gets ripped out to make room.
  • Something worth reusingWe prefer to build components we can maintain and reuse, for example as an extension of one of our products.
  • A person stays in chargeAnything uncertain or sensitive goes to your team.

If what you need is general software development with no AI workflow in it, we’ll tell you we’re not the right team.

How we deliver

  1. Discover the workflowWe sit with the people who do the work and map the steps, the data and the systems involved.
  2. ConfigureWe design the system around how you work: the rules, the wording and the cases that always go to a person.
  3. IntegrateWe connect it to the systems you already use, so nobody types the same information twice.
  4. LaunchWe test with real scenarios, then put it into production with your team.
  5. ImproveWe stay close after launch and adjust it as your business changes.
Who owns whatCode written only for you is yours. Where the work extends one of our products, that part stays in the product and you use it under the product terms.
