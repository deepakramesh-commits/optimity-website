What we take on

It’s selective. These are the things we look for before we say yes.

  • A real operational workflowSomething your team does every week, such as handling one kind of customer request or moving information between two systems.
  • Your existing systems stayWe build around the systems you already run, and agree each connection before we start.
  • Something worth reusingWe prefer to build components we can reuse, for example as an extension of one of our products.
  • A person stays in chargeBefore it goes live, we agree with you where a person needs to check its work.

If what you need is general software development with no AI workflow in it, we’ll tell you we’re not the right team.

How we deliver

  1. Discover the workflowWe sit with the people who do the work and map the steps, the data and the systems involved.
  2. ConfigureWe design the system around how you work: the rules, the wording and the cases that always go to a person.
  3. IntegrateWe connect it to the tools you already use, so there’s less retyping between systems.
  4. LaunchWe test it against real situations and roll it out with your team.
