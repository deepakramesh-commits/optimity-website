Every call answered, day or night

Concierge AI is an AI receptionist for businesses that take bookings by phone. It answers every call, day or night, and books appointments into Google Calendar. Every call and booking shows up in your dashboard, and when a person needs to follow up, Concierge AI sends the details to your team.

What it does when it answers

  • Answers the phoneAt 2 am or while you’re with a customer, it asks the questions you’d ask and takes down the details.
  • Books the appointmentStraight into Google Calendar, when the caller wants one and there’s a slot that fits.
  • Moves an appointmentWhen a caller’s plans change, it reschedules the booking.
  • Logs it in your dashboardEvery call and every booking in one place, so you can see what came in while you were working.
  • Hands over to a personWhen a person needs to follow up, Concierge AI sends the details to your team.
