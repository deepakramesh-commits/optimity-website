Who it’s for

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs and handymen, from one-truck owner-operators to shops running a few crews, where the phone rings most while everyone is out on jobs.

The problem

A homeowner’s water heater starts leaking at 7 in the morning. They call you while you’re in a crawlspace on another job, get voicemail, and call the next plumber in the search results.

How it works, for one call

  1. The call

    At 11:48 pm a homeowner calls about a leaking water heater and asks for someone this week. Halfway through, she says water is spreading across the garage floor. Trade Concierge AI takes the address and what’s happening and sends the details to your team.

  2. The booking

    It also books a visit to look at the heater properly, in Google Calendar. Routine calls, like a faucet swap or an outlet that stopped working, get booked the same way.

  3. The dashboard

    Next morning the dashboard shows every call from the night, the leak included: who called, what they needed, when the details went to your team and the visit booked for Thursday.

What stays with youWhen a person needs to follow up, such as on a question about price, Trade Concierge AI sends the details to your team. It isn’t a substitute for 911.

How it fits your systems

During setup we connect it to your phone line and Google Calendar. If your crews work from another scheduling tool, that’s a custom integration we can scope and build.
