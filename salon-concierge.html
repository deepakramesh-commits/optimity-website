Bookings handled while every chair is full

Salon Concierge AI is the AI receptionist for nail and hair salons. It answers every call, day or night, books appointments into Google Calendar and moves them when a client’s plans change. When a person needs to follow up, such as on a question about price, it sends the details to your team. Every call and booking is in your dashboard.

Who it’s for

Nail salons, lash and beauty studios, spas, hair salons, barbershops and independent stylists, whether you have a front desk or rent a chair, where most calls are someone trying to book.

The problem

It’s Saturday and every chair is full. A regular calls to move her appointment, a new client calls to ask about color, and neither of them leaves a message.

A nail client moves her appointment

  1. The call

    A regular calls at 8:52 am to move Saturday’s gel fill. Salon Concierge AI offers the next openings and moves it to Sunday at 10. Then she asks whether her sister can come in at the same time.

  2. The booking

    It books her sister in at 10, next to her, and Saturday at 11 opens up for someone else. Both changes go into Google Calendar.

  3. The dashboard

    Check the call record between clients: the gel fill moved to Sunday, the pedicure added next to it, and Saturday at 11 freed up and booked again by 9:41 am.

A new client asks what color costs

  1. The call

    On a busy Saturday afternoon a new client calls and asks what a balayage costs. Salon Concierge AI sends her question to your team so a person can follow up on the price, and offers to book her in while she’s on the phone.

  2. The booking

    She takes Jordan’s Tuesday 5:30 opening for a cut and blow-dry, and the appointment goes into Google Calendar.

  3. The dashboard

    The call record shows what she asked, the question sent to your team and her appointment with Jordan on Tuesday.
