Bookings handled while every chair is full

Salon Concierge AI is the AI receptionist for nail and hair salons. It books clients with the technician or stylist they ask for and moves an appointment when their plans change. When someone asks what a service costs, it books a consultation or passes the question to your team. Every call is in your dashboard.

Who it’s for

Nail salons, lash and beauty studios, spas, hair salons, barbershops and independent stylists, whether you have a front desk or rent a chair, where most calls are someone trying to book.

The problem

It’s Saturday and every chair is full. A regular calls to move her appointment, a new client calls to ask about color, and neither of them leaves a message.

A nail client moves her appointment

  1. The call

    A regular calls at 8:52 am to move Saturday’s gel fill. Salon Concierge AI finds her appointment, offers Mia’s next openings and moves it to Sunday at 10. Then she asks whether her sister can come in at the same time.

  2. The booking

    It books her sister with Ana at 10, next to her, and Saturday at 11 opens up for someone else. Both changes go into your booking system while she’s still on the phone.

  3. The dashboard

    Check the call record between clients: the gel fill moved to Sunday, the pedicure added next to it, and Saturday at 11 freed up and booked again by 9:41 am.

A new client asks what color costs

  1. The call

    On a busy Saturday afternoon a new client calls and asks what a balayage costs. Salon Concierge AI tells her the colorist sets the price at a short consultation and offers to book one.

  2. The booking

    She takes Jordan’s Tuesday 5:30 opening. The consultation goes into your booking system with the service she asked about, and the price question goes to your team in case they want to call first.

  3. The dashboard

    The call record shows what she asked, that no price was given, the consultation with Jordan on Tuesday, and the note passed to your front desk.

What stays with youPrices, color corrections, extensions, bookings for three or more people, bridal parties and complaints go to you or your team. It only books people who are free, for services you’ve allowed.

How it fits your systems

We set it up with your booking system and each technician’s or stylist’s services and hours, and mark which services need a consultation before they can be booked.
