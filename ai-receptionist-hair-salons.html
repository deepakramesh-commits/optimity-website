Who it’s for

Hair salons, barbershops and independent stylists, whether you have a front desk or rent a chair, where most calls are someone trying to book.

The problem

There’s a client in your chair and two more waiting. The phone rings three times in ten minutes. One of those calls was a new client asking about color, and they didn’t leave a message.

How it works, for one call

  1. The call

    On a busy Saturday afternoon a new client calls and asks what a balayage costs. The AI Receptionist tells her the colorist sets the price at a short consultation and offers to book one.

  2. The booking

    She takes Jordan’s Tuesday 5:30 opening. The consultation goes into your booking system with the service she asked about, and the price question goes to your team in case they want to call first.

  3. The dashboard

    Her consultation sits in the afternoon’s list next to the cuts and trims it booked, along with an extensions question it passed to you.

What stays with youColor corrections, extensions and anything about price go to you or your team. It only books stylists who are free, for services you’ve allowed.

How it fits your systems

We connect it to your booking system with each stylist’s hours, and mark which services need a consultation before they can be booked.
