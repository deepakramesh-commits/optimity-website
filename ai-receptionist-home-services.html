Who it’s for

Plumbers, electricians, HVAC techs and handymen, from one-truck owner-operators to shops running a few crews, where the phone rings most while everyone is out on jobs.

The problem

A homeowner’s water heater starts leaking at 7 in the morning. They call you while you’re in a crawlspace on another job, get voicemail, and call the next plumber in the search results.

How it works, for one call

  1. The call

    At 11:48 pm a homeowner calls about a leaking water heater and asks for someone this week. Halfway through, water starts spreading across the garage floor. The AI Receptionist marks the call urgent and passes the address and what’s happening to your on-call tech.

  2. The booking

    It also books a visit to look at the heater properly, at a time you’ve opened for that kind of job. Routine calls, like a faucet swap or an outlet that stopped working, get booked the same way.

  3. The dashboard

    Next morning the leak is at the top of your dashboard: who called, what they needed, when it went to your on-call tech, and the visit booked for Thursday. The rest of the night’s calls sit underneath.

What stays with youIt doesn’t price jobs or decide what’s wrong. On an urgent call it passes the details on without saying when someone will arrive, and the visits it books are only at times you’ve opened. It isn’t a substitute for 911: during setup you decide what it tells callers who report a gas smell, carbon monoxide or sparking.

How it fits your systems

During setup we connect it to your phone line and the schedule your crews work from, and agree who gets urgent calls on which nights.
