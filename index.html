Move Concierge AI answers when you can’t

It’s 7:40 on a Monday evening. Someone moving a three-bedroom house on the 14th calls your office, gets voicemail, and rings the next mover on their list. Move Concierge AI takes those calls, asks what your estimator would ask, and has the enquiry waiting for your team in the morning.

  1. The call comes in

    On the calls you route to it: after hours, at weekends, or when every line is busy. Callers talk normally and can interrupt.

  2. It asks about the move

    Moving date, where from and where to, the size of the home, stairs and lift access, and large or fragile items such as a piano, artwork, gym equipment or a safe. It doesn’t give binding prices or promise dates.

  3. It writes up the enquiry

    Contact details and every answer go into one record, with the call recording and transcript attached.

  4. It books the next step

    Where you’ve set it up, it books a video or in-person survey in your estimators’ calendar, or schedules a callback, and sends the caller a summary by SMS or WhatsApp.

  5. Your team takes it from there

    The enquiry reaches your staff by email or in your moving software or CRM, depending on what we connect at setup. Complaints, unusual requests and anything it can’t answer go straight to a person.

Each morning you can see what came in overnight: how many enquiries, which surveys were booked and which callers still need a call back.
