Questions to ask before you choose

  • Will it work with my setup?It depends on the product. Concierge AI books appointments into Google Calendar. Nexus AI connects to ERPNext, HubSpot, PostgreSQL and supported document sources. Property Connect AI can call back eligible leads that come through the forms and sources we connect. Anything else is a custom integration we can scope and build, and we confirm what your setup needs before anything is connected.
  • Can my team take over?Each product hands over in its own way. When a person needs to follow up, Concierge AI sends the details to your team. Property Connect AI briefs an agent privately before it joins a serious buyer to the call. Nexus AI only makes a change in a connected system after you’ve turned that action on and approved it.
  • Can I see what happened?Concierge AI shows every call and booking in a dashboard. Nexus AI can cite the documents behind an answer; for ERP, CRM and database answers, how far you can trace the source depends on the connector and the question. With Property Connect AI, where leads land depends on the CRM connection we build with you.
