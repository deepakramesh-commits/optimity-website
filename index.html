  • It works with what you already runYour phone line, calendar, booking system, CRM or ERP stays where it is. We confirm what your setup needs before anything is connected.

How a product goes live

  1. Look at the workflowWe listen to how your calls or questions are handled today and agree what the product does and when it hands over to a person.
  2. ConfigureQuestions, wording, business rules, and the situations that always go to your team.
  3. ConnectTo the phone line, calendar, CRM or business systems you already use, where the product supports them.
  4. LaunchWe test with real scenarios, then go live with your team watching the first conversations.
  5. ImproveWe review the calls, answers and outcomes with you and adjust the setup.
