Who it’s for

Nail salons, lash and brow studios, and spas, where every technician has a client in the chair and the front desk is either busy or doesn’t exist.

The problem

It’s Saturday morning and every station is full. A regular calls to move her appointment, the phone rings out, and her slot stays empty while someone who wanted it books somewhere else.

How it works, for one call

  1. The call

    A regular calls at 8:52 am to move Saturday’s gel fill. The AI Receptionist finds her appointment, offers Mia’s next openings and moves it to Sunday at 10. Then she asks whether her sister can come in at the same time.

  2. The booking

    It books her sister with Ana at 10, next to her, and Saturday at 11 opens up for someone else. Both changes go into your booking system while she’s still on the phone.

  3. The dashboard

    Check the dashboard between clients: the gel fill shows as moved, the pedicure as new, and Saturday at 11 as open again for someone else to book.

What stays with youPrices, group and bridal bookings, and anything about a reaction or a complaint go to you.

How it fits your systems

We set it up with your booking system and each technician’s services and hours, so it only offers slots a technician can actually take.
