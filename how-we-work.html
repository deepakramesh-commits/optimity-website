What that looks like on one order

An example of a flow we map on a teardown. Same WhatsApp, same ERPNext, with the retyping taken out.

Today, by hand

  1. CustomerSends the order on WhatsApp
  2. SalesRetypes it into ERPNext
  3. SalesPhones the warehouse to check stock
  4. AccountsRaises the invoice from a spreadsheet
  5. AccountsChases payment from calendar reminders

After we build it

  1. CustomerSends the order on WhatsApp
  2. SystemReads the message and drafts the sales order in ERPNext
  3. SalesChecks the draft and approves it
  4. SystemChecks stock and raises the invoice
  5. SystemFlags the invoice when it passes 30 days

Example workflow, not a client's.
