What it does on every call

  • Answers the phoneAt 2 am or while you’re with a customer, it asks the questions you’d ask and takes down the details.
  • Books the appointmentStraight into your booking system, in a slot that’s actually open.
  • Logs it in your dashboardEvery call and every booking in one place, so you can see what came in while you were working.
  • Hands over when it shouldEmergencies, complaints and anything it can’t answer go to you or your team, the way you set it up.

What it won’t do

It won’t quote a price, promise a time you haven’t offered or agree to an exception. Those stay with you.
