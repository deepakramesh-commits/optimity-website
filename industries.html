Four kinds of systems, in any sector

Workflow automation

Software that does the multi-step work your team does by hand: reading from the ERP, writing to the CRM, moving the order along without the retyping.

Document intelligence

Contracts, invoices, referrals and manuals read into structured, searchable records that your team approves instead of retyping.

Decision support

The morning questions answered before anyone asks: what's about to go wrong, which account needs a call, what to reorder.

Compliance & audit

Checks that run all the time against your own rules and regulations, with a trail you can hand to an auditor.
