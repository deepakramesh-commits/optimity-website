Who it’s for

Operations, finance and sales managers who need figures from ERPNext, Odoo, HubSpot or a PostgreSQL database, or answers from contracts and policies, without waiting for someone to build a report.

The problem

It’s Monday morning and the sales manager wants to know which customers have an overdue invoice and an open order. The answer is in two systems, and the person who usually exports it is on leave.

How it works

Ask about your systems, your documents or your database, and Nexus AI answers from the right source.

Questions about orders, invoices, inventory and pipeline

Two-way access to sales pipelines, inventory, invoices and purchase orders, with summaries such as unfulfilled orders and overdue invoices, and charts inside the conversation.

  • Live connectors for ERPNext, Odoo and HubSpot
  • Bar, line, pie and donut charts generated in the conversation
  • Cross-system reconciliation and operational alerts

Questions across documents, contracts and reports

Answers from your knowledge base, PDFs, spreadsheets, operating procedures and compliance documents, each with the passage it came from.

  • Semantic search (RAG) on Qdrant
  • Citations with the source snippet, so every answer can be checked
  • A separate knowledge base for each team or organization

Plain English to SQL, for people who don’t write SQL

Executives and operations managers query production PostgreSQL databases in plain English. Queries run in strict read-only sandboxes, and results can be sorted and exported.

Example query

SELECT count(*) FROM (
  SELECT customer_id FROM orders
  WHERE created_at >= date_trunc('quarter', now())
  GROUP BY customer_id HAVING count(*) > 1
) repeat_customers;
