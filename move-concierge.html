What it does

Takes the enquiry

  • Captures origin, destination, move dates, home size, lift access and special packing needs in conversation
  • Itemises the inventory room by room, by voice
  • Flags heavy or fragile items: pianos, artwork, gym equipment, safes
  • Estimates weight and cubic footage ranges, and sends the quote summary by SMS or WhatsApp

Books the survey

  • Checks surveyor availability and books video or in-person visits on the first call
  • Works with your dispatch tool, Google Calendar or booking system
  • Handles rescheduling, cancellations and arrival confirmations
  • Sends a pre-move checklist on WhatsApp 48 hours before, and updates customers on truck dispatch and crew arrival

Reports back

  • Audio recordings and searchable transcripts, with sentiment analysis
  • Lead qualification scores and conversion funnels
  • A daily summary of inbound leads, bookings and lost leads
  • Webhook sync to MoveOps, Supermove, HubSpot or your own database
