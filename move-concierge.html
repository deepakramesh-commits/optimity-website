Who it’s for

Independent moving companies with a busy phone line, where the owner, general manager or office manager decides how calls are handled and there’s already a phone line and a calendar in place.

The problem

Moving customers call in the evening, on weekends, and in the middle of the day when everyone is on another line. By the time someone calls back in the morning, the customer may already have booked an estimate elsewhere.

How it works, from the call to your team

  1. The call comes in

    On the calls you route to it: after hours, on weekends, or when every line is busy. Callers talk normally and can interrupt.

  2. It asks about the move

    Moving date, where from and where to, the size of the home, stairs and elevator access, and large or fragile items such as a piano, artwork, gym equipment or a safe. A question about price goes to your team with the rest of the details.

  3. It writes up the lead

    Contact details and the caller’s answers go into one lead record in your dashboard.

  4. It books the estimate

    It books a virtual or in-home estimate in your estimators’ Google Calendar.

  5. Your team takes it from there

    When a person needs to follow up, Move Concierge AI sends the details to your team. In this sample, the caller also asked about storage between moves.

What you can see

Your dashboard shows every call and booking, so the office can see first thing in the morning what came in overnight.
